Moving into Product Management in CRM Consulting at EY (Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Platform) taught me how to turn stakeholder needs into requirements that engineering could actually build and QA could actually test.

Along the way, I also completed a postgraduate program in Data Science, including a capstone on adversarial attacks against image classifiers. Today, I'm combining that business grounding, requirements thinking, and applied AI interest to move into AI Technical Product Management roles.
